# Project 2: Tableau

## What this is
Used Tableau Public to dig into subscription data for a fictional SaaS business, 5,000 synthetic customers. Covers retention, revenue retention, and churn.

## Data files
- `subscriptions.csv` (from `generate_data.py`): customer id, plan tier, start/end dates, MRR, region, acquisition channel, downgrade flag and date
- `mrr_history.csv` (from `generate_mrr_history.py`): monthly MRR per customer
- `nrr_waterfall.csv` (from `nrr_waterfall.py`): New/Expansion/Contraction/Churned totals by month

## What's in the dashboards

**Cohort Retention Matrix**
- Groups customers by signup month, tracks how many stick around each month after
- Used a WINDOW_SUM table calc to get this right, an LOD approach kept showing 100% retention everywhere which was wrong

**NRR Waterfall**
- Breaks down monthly MRR movement into New, Expansion, Contraction, and Churned
- Numbers reconcile cleanly: for Feb 2024, starting MRR of $17,140 plus $15,770 new plus $740 expansion minus $640 contraction minus $1,350 churned lands exactly on $31,660 ending MRR

**Churn Trends**
- Splits churn into two types and trends both monthly, side by side
- Hard churn (red line): customers whose subscription actually ended
- Soft churn (green line): customers who downgraded but are still active, not gone yet
- Had to add a downgrade_date field to the data generator for this one, the original downgrade flag was just a yes/no with no date attached, so there was nothing to trend it against
